### How to Use This Index

Each topic recurs across all four passes by design (§0's spiral model): Pass 1 builds the intuition, Pass 2 explains the mechanism, Pass 3 diagnoses its production failure modes, and Pass 4 shows it integrated into one evolving real system. When a production incident or a design decision touches one of the topics above, this index is the fastest way to locate every relevant section across the whole handbook, rather than searching a single part in isolation.
